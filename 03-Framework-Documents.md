## 1. THE BAHAMAS

### ✅ BHS-FW-01 — Public Finance Management Act 2023 (Part VIII)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Direct PDF** | `https://frcbahamas.gov.bs/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Public-Finance-Management-Act-2023.pdf` — **200 OK. 734 KB, 106 pages, opens on "ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS / PART I – PRELIMINARY". This is the complete Act.** |
| **Host** | Fiscal Responsibility Council, The Bahamas (`frcbahamas.gov.bs`) — the reliable host |
| **Official legal site** | `https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/` ⚠️ **403 Forbidden to automated requests.** Open manually in a browser |
| **⚠️ Filename trap** | The *same* folder on the *same* host also serves `Public-Finance-Management-Act-2023-Appointed-Day-Notice-2023.pdf`. That file is a **3-page gazette commencement notice**, not the Act. Confirm you have the 106-page file before quoting a section number — quoting "Part VIII" out of a commencement notice is the kind of error that goes badly in a viva |
| **Amendment to check** | Public Finance Management **(Amendment)** Act 2023 = **No. 15 of 2023**. Check whether any provision you score was subsequently amended |
| **Feeds** | D1.2, D1.3, D2.1, D3.1, D3.2, D4.1, D7.3 |
| **Status** | ✅ Verified live — full text, page count and opening page all confirmed |

**What to look for in Part VIII:**
- The **definition** of a government corporation / statutory body — establishes which entities are in scope (also compare with the OECD's own definition, cited in `02-…Benchmark-Map.md` Part A)
- **Who must report**, **to whom**, **by when** — this is a **D3** test
- **Performance targets or corporate plans** and who approves them — **D2**
- Any provision on **consequences for non-compliance** — search Part VIII and the Schedules specifically for *default*, *offence*, *penalty*, *failure to comply*. **Finding none is a finding** — record the negative search result explicitly
- **Board appointment and removal** of the CEO/General Manager — **D5.2**, and check whether a removal ground and process are specified (see the 2-vs-3 test)
- **Audit and audit-report timelines** — **D7**

> **Also worth locating:** the Public Finance Management **Regulations** made under the Act, and the **Fiscal Responsibility Act** framework. Subordinate legislation is where reporting deadlines usually live, and it is where students most often forget to look.

| **Feeds** | D1.2, D1.3, D2.1, D3.1, D3.2, D5.1, D7.3 |
| **Status** | ✅ Statute page verified; title, operational date and last amendment all confirmed on-page |

**What to look for:**
- The **definition of a "public body"** — Jamaica's definition is broad and will shape your sample
- **Corporate plans and performance targets**: who prepares them, who approves them, whether annual **performance reports** against the plan are mandatory, and **what happens if they are not submitted** — this is a direct **D5.1** test and one of the most important single provisions in your corpus
- **Annual reports and deadlines to Parliament**
- **Board appointment, tenure and removal** provisions
- **Ministerial directions** power — a power to direct is *not* a consequence mechanism; do not score it as D5
- Locate the **Public Bodies (Management and Accountability) Regulations**, which often contain the actual deadlines

> **Jamaica-specific evidence link:** the **Public Bodies Estimates** published annually with the national budget are a rare and valuable document type — they give you *actual* SOE financial data for the Disclosure Audit and for de facto scoring. Look for them alongside Jamaica's Estimates of Expenditure.

|---|---|
| **Direct PDF** ⭐ | `https://oag.gov.bb/attachments/Public%20Finance%20Management%20Act,%202019-1.pdf` — **200 OK, verified** |
| **Landing page** | `https://oag.gov.bb/` ✅ 200 — browsable by year at `/Laws/Annuals/[year]/Acts-Annual/` |
| **⚠️ Naming trap** | **`oag.gov.bb` is the Office of the *Attorney General*** — Barbados's legislation portal. It is **not** the Auditor General. The Auditor General is the **Barbados Audit Office at `bao.gov.bb`** (see `04-Evidence-Documents.md`). Two different agencies, near-identical acronyms — cite the right one, or your D7 evidence gets attributed to the wrong institution |
| **Why it matters** | The Auditor General's own 2024 report cites **"Section 77(5) of the Public Finance Management Act, 2019-1"** as its statutory audit basis — confirming this Act's direct relevance to SOE financial reporting and audit obligations |
| **Status** | ✅ **Direct PDF verified live** |
| **Feeds** | D1.2, D1.3, D2.1, D3.1, D3.2, D4.1, D7.2, D7.3 |

**What to look for:**
- Scope: which state enterprises and statutory corporations the Act covers, and **which it does not** — the coverage boundary is itself a D1 finding
- **Reporting timelines and default provisions**
- Provisions on **government companies** and **statutory corporations** — Barbados' PFM Act is comparatively modern (2019) and tends to have more explicit timelines than older Caribbean statutes. **If Barbados scores higher on D3, that is an interesting finding — check whether the timelines carry consequences or only deadlines**
- Auditor General's powers, access rights and **reporting timeline to Parliament** — **D7.2, D7.3**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Direct PDF** ⭐ | `https://www.finance.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/State-Enterprise-Performance-Monitoring-Manual-2011.pdf` — **200 OK, verified** (~2.6 MB) |
| **Ministry of Finance** | `https://www.finance.gov.tt/` ✅ 200 — **not bot-blocked.** The most automation-friendly official site of the four countries, so you can verify links yourself here |
| **Also on that host** | Companies Act `…/2019/07/The-Companies-Act.pdf` ✅ · PSIP 2025 `…/2025/08/PSIP-Trinidad2025-Final-Digital.pdf` ✅ · SEIP 2026 `…/2025/10/SEIP-2026-for-web.pdf` (locate) · **List of state enterprises and statutory boards** `…/2024/01/MOF-Company-and-Statutory-Board-Listing.pdf` (locate — **this is your sampling frame**) |
| **Contents (confirmed)** | General Operating Framework · Reporting Relationships (Cabinet / Minister of Finance / Investments Division / Line Ministries / Boards) · Corporate Governance Guidelines (director appointment, AGMs, auditor appointment, procurement, dividend policy) · Performance Monitoring (strategic plans, budgets, risk management, financial statements) · Oversight & Compliance |
| **Status** | ✅ **Direct PDF verified live** |

**Why this document matters more than the others:** T&T is the **only one of your four cases with a dedicated SOE performance-monitoring instrument** rather than general public-finance legislation. That makes T&T your natural **best-case comparison**:

> If the Consequence Gap is still present in Trinidad and Tobago — the case with the most developed monitoring framework — then the gap is attributable to features of the environment rather than to legislative indifference. **That is the strongest single test your P2 has.**

**What to look for:**
- The structure of the monitoring relationship: SOE → line ministry → Ministry of Finance
- **Reporting frequency** (monthly? quarterly?) and the standard reporting pack
- **Forum of consultations / performance review meetings** — a real governance feature; T&T's framework is unusually explicit about review meetings
- **What happens when an SOE does not report or misses targets.** If the Manual specifies a review process but no consequence, that is a **de jure 2** at D5.1 and it will be one of your most important single data points
- Whether the Manual is **binding** (issued under statute/regulation) or **administrative guidance** — this determines whether the whole document scores 1 or 2/3. **Establish this explicitly and cite the empowering provision**

## 5. Cross-cutting: get the framework documents in force at the right date

| Trap | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Score the current framework, not the famous one** | Score against the law **in force today**, and footnote the commencement date and any amendment |
| **Watch for recent replacement** | The Bahamas' **PFM Act 2023** replaced older financial administration legislation. If a secondary source discusses the "Financial Administration and Audit Act," you may be reading superseded law. **Check the year on every citation before you score it** |
| **Subordinate legislation** | Reporting deadlines and forms are usually in **Regulations**, not the principal Act. Miss them and you will score D3 too low and blame the small state for what is only your own incomplete search. **Search for Regulations explicitly for every country** |
| **Coverage boundaries differ** | Each country defines its SOE population differently. Jamaica's "public bodies," Barbados' "state enterprises," T&T's "state enterprises," the Bahamas' "government corporations." **Map these definitions against each other before scoring D1** — otherwise you are comparing unlike populations |
